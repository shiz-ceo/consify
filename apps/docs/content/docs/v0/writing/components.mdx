## Video and images

`<Video src="..." title="..." />` embeds a video with controls. Every image, GIF and video gets a thin border that takes the tint of the media under it. Use `<img plain />` to leave one without.

## Blog components

The blog adds `Authors`, `Expand`, `PR`, `Benchmark`, `Figure`, `Embed` and `CTA`. See [Blog components](/docs/v0/blog/components).
