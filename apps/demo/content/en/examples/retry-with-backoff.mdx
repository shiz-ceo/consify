---
title: Retry with backoff
description: Retry a failing job with exponential delays.
level: beginner
tags: [retries, jobs]
date: 2026-08-01
---

A job that fails is retried. Give it a number of attempts and a backoff:

```ts
await payments.add({ orderId: "o_1" }, { retries: 5, backoff: "exponential" });
```

The delay doubles with every attempt, with some jitter so retries do not arrive together.
